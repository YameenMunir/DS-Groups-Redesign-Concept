About DS Groups

Built on values of hard work, integrity and community, we take pride in delivering consistent quality and service across every brand we operate.

Today DS Groups operates a national network of franchise locations across two leading fast-food brands, Domino’s and German Doner Kebab (GDK), as well as a premier fitness franchise.

Our mission is to deliver outstanding customer experiences, build lasting relationships based on trust, and promote sustainable growth for our team and partners.

Our journey

1999

Dip Dhillon’s manager tells him a store is for sale. He goes all in, and quickly turns the store around.

Our people

Success stories

More than 90% of Domino’s store managers and area managers started out as a delivery driver or a pizza maker.

Lucy Harman: from answering phones at 16 to owning a store

Lucy began at our Whitstable branch, two evenings a week. She rose from shift manager to store manager, then, with our support and mentoring, became a franchisee and opened her first store in Hayling Island, Hampshire. She is Domino’s first female Homegrown Hero.

Lucy’s story

John Prior: owning his own gym

After ten years as regional manager of our Snap Fitness franchise, John achieved his dream of owning his own gym franchise with our mentoring. In 2024 he was awarded CEO One Team of the Year.
